## What Metrology and Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) Protocol Qualifies YIXING Equipment Before Shipment?

Before releasing final payment or shipping any labeling machine, tape applicator, or box folder, buyers should require a **1,000-unit continuous FAT protocol** using buyer-supplied consumables (actual parts, rolls of 3M/PI tape or labels, and die-cut cartons) recorded on unedited single-take video. Because YIXING does not hold third-party ISO 9001 or IEC 17025 lab accreditation, measurable pre-shipment metrology replaces generic marketing assurances.

1. **Pre-Order Free Sample Evaluation:** Courier actual containers, PCBs, wires, or folded blanks plus minimum two full rolls of your exact adhesive tape/labels to Shipai Town, Dongguan; verify scratch-free surface handling, liner-peel stability, clean pneumatic cutting, and achievable real-world throughput (not peak rated speed on empty cycles).
2. **Pre-Shipment Component & Electrical Inspection:** Verify PLC (`Panasonic`), HMI (`MCGS` / `WECON`), optical sensors (`Panasonic` / `Leuze`), emergency-stop circuits, E-stop relay schematics, and voltage/plug specification (`110V/220V/380V`) against agreed PO BOM; request third-party field inspection (`SGS` / `TÜV` / buyer-appointed QC) if CE compliance evaluation for your local EU jurisdiction is needed at destination.
3. **1,000-Unit Unedited FAT Run & Optical/Gage Measurement:**
   * **Vision Labeling (`±0.05 mm` class):** Sample `n ≥ 30`–`50` consecutive units from a 1,000-piece continuous run; measure X, Y, and rotational `θ` offset using calibrated optical vision comparator or coordinate metrology under fixed jig indexing; require defect/jam rate `≤ 0.2%` and document `Cp / Cpk`.
   * **Industrial 3M / PI Tape Application (`500 mm/s` class):** Verify sustained tape feed without adhesive buildup on guide rollers/blades, complete release-liner peel/rewind without tearing, zero edge lift or air pockets, and repeatability within `±0.5 mm` (CCD vision) or `±1–2 mm` (mechanical/inline) on customer parts.
   * **Box Folding & Integrated Lines (`YX-KB/CS/CH`):** Run `≥ 500–1,000` continuous cartons of customer board stock (corrugated / laminated SBS) confirming lock engagement integrity, squareness, zero surface scuffing on laminated art paper, and synchronized handoff into downstream tape application or corner-seal labeling.
